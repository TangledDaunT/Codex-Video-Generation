## Editorial choices (hypotheses, not ranking facts)

For the user's adopted writing approach, five retention patterns, curiosity/payoff mapping, and motion-stack requirements, read [storytelling-motion.md](storytelling-motion.md) before scripting. These are editorial techniques, not verified Instagram ranking rules or a guarantee that viewers will finish a video.

Make the subject and reason to watch apparent immediately. Choose an opening tied to this audience and supported by the actual story: a recognizable pain point, a credible outcome, a specific question, a surprising visual, or an honest contrast. Deliver the promised payoff, cut redundant setup, and keep narration conversational. Show the result when it helps explain the value. Avoid deceptive clickbait, unverifiable superlatives, or a vague “you won’t believe this” promise.

If useful, propose two or three brief opening variants while keeping the approved story unchanged. Explain why the chosen hook fits the audience. Do not generate extra variants or spend credits unless covered by permission. Use feedback to change only the opening/edit when appropriate.

Default vertical 1080×1920 is the user's requested export specification, not a claimed algorithm boost. Keep key text and subjects clear of interface overlays; verify current safe-area guidance or preview instead of inventing universal pixel margins. Captions should accurately follow the spoken audio.
